The Dashboards
📍
Snapshot of Homelessness
A monthly count of people experiencing homelessness
▼

Shows the Annual Point-in-Time (PIT) count for the CoC as submitted through HDX 2.0, alongside a monthly count of individuals experiencing homelessness.

🏘️
Current Living Situations
Where clients are staying, by project type
▼

Shows last month's Current Living Situation for each client, by project type — reported for Street Outreach and Coordinated Entry only — presented through the Current Living Situation HMIS Assessment.

Note: The Balance of State CoC will not have Street Outreach (SO), so this category reflects Lincoln only.

🔑
Program Exits to Permanent Housing
Clients exiting to permanent housing
▼

Shows last month's exits to permanent housing only. Destination names are shortened for clarity — for example, instead of "Living with Family in a permanent tenure," this is shown simply as "Living with Family."

🏠
Housed in Supportive Housing Programs
Clients housed in a supportive housing program
▼

Shows a count of individuals who came from a homeless situation and are currently housed in one of the CoC's housing projects, updated monthly.

🚶
Street Outreach Efforts to Housing Placement
Engagement through outreach services
Only in Lincoln ▼

Individuals engaged in Street Outreach services, including those who have subsequently obtained housing.

📦
Program Utilization
How much of the housing inventory is in use
▼

Shows the Annual Housing Inventory Count (HIC) for the CoC as submitted through HDX 2.0, alongside current utilization, updated monthly.

🔗
Coordinated Entry Efforts to Housing Resolution
Families in Coordinated Entry & how cases resolve
▼

Total number of families in Coordinated Entry, and the outcomes of successful exits to permanent housing, including CoC housing placements and self-resolutions. Updated monthly.

🆕
First-Time Homelessness
System Performance Measure 5.0
▼

Based on System Performance Measure 5.0 (SPM 5.0), this shows how many individuals experienced homelessness for the first time that month, with no homeless episode recorded in HMIS within the past 24 months.

💰
Rental Assistance
Households, adults, and children receiving support
▼

Part of Lincoln's Homeless Prevention and Supported Housing program. Tracks more than just households — also the number of clients, adults, and children who received rental assistance or are currently housed through the program.

🎖️
Veterans Inflow & Outflow
Veterans entering & exiting housing
▼

Part of Lincoln's veteran housing programs. Shows veterans entering and exiting housing projects each month, broken out by project type.

Data for planning and community insights. Figures shown in the live dashboards are updated automatically each month. This overview page is a concept summary and does not itself display monthly figures — visit each dashboard directly for current numbers.
